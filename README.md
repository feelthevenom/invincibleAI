# Gym AI 🏋️‍♂️🥗

**Gym AI** is your all-in-one intelligent fitness companion. Whether you're lifting heavy at the gym, tracking your daily nutrition, or looking for expert coaching advice, Gym AI helps you reach your goals using advanced Artificial Intelligence that works both online and completely offline.

---

## ✨ Key Features

### 📋 Smart Workout Tracker
*   **Ready-to-Use Routines:** Start immediately with built-in Push/Pull/Legs, Full Body, or Cardio routines.
*   **Custom Editor:** Create your own routines, reorder exercises, and save your favorites.
*   **Guided Exercises:** Access over 700 exercises with GIF animations and step-by-step instructions.
*   **Progressive Overload:** The app remembers your last weights and reps, suggesting targets for your next session to keep you improving.
*   **Cardio & Weights:** Track everything from running timers to heavy squats in one place.

### 🍎 Intelligent Nutrition & Diet
*   **Meal Logging:** Track Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner, and Snacks with personalized calorie budgets.
*   **Snap & Log:** Don't want to type? Just take a photo of your plate, and the AI will estimate the calories and macros for you.
*   **Massive Food Database:** Search through thousands of items, including local Indian foods and packaged products via Open Food Facts.
*   **AI Food Lookup:** If you can't find a niche item, the AI will find the nutrition info for you.

### 🤖 Your Personal AI Coach
*   **Daily Insights:** Get actionable advice on your diet and exercise habits right on your dashboard.
*   **Interactive Chat:** Ask your coach anything! "How do I get more protein?" or "Suggest a 20-minute home workout."
*   **Tutorial Generator:** If an exercise is missing steps, the AI can generate a custom guide for you instantly.

## 🚀 Getting Started

### 1. Installation
1.  Open the project in **Android Studio**.
2.  Connect your Android phone or start an emulator.
3.  Click the **Run ▶** button to install Gym AI.

### 2. First Launch
When you first open the app, you'll go through a quick **Onboarding**:
*   Tell the app your goals (Lose weight, Build muscle, etc.).
*   Enter your age, weight, and height.
*   Select how many days a week you want to work out.
*   The app will automatically calculate your daily calorie and protein targets!

---

## 🛠 Setting up the AI (Online or Offline)

Gym AI gives you the freedom to choose how you want to use the AI. Go to **Sidebar → Settings → AI Settings** to configure:

### Option A: Online AI (Fastest & Most Detailed)
If you have an internet connection, you can use powerful cloud models:
1.  **Get a Key:** Obtain a free API key from [Google AI Studio (Gemini)](https://aistudio.google.com/apikey) or [Groq](https://console.groq.com/keys).
2.  **Add to App:** Tap "Add API Key" in the app settings and paste it in.
3.  **Test:** Tap "Test API Connection" to make sure you're ready to go.

### Option B: Offline AI (Complete Privacy, No Internet)
Perfect for when you're at the gym with no signal or want 100% privacy:
1.  Set your Provider to **Offline**.
2.  **Download a Model:** Tap the Download button next to the "Gemma" models (~2-3 GB).
3.  **Use Anywhere:** Once downloaded, your AI Coach and Food Analysis will work even in airplane mode!
